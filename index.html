ABOUT ME

Building a bridge between IT and industry.

I’m a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from City University, Dhaka, with a goal of building my career in Industrial IT. I’m especially interested in industrial networking, OT infrastructure, and the technologies that help industrial systems communicate reliably.

I’m strengthening my foundation in networking concepts such as TCP/IP, Ethernet, VLANs, routing, and switching, while learning how these fundamentals connect to industrial automation and modern connected facilities.

My previous work experiences have helped me develop communication, teamwork, attention to detail, and problem-solving skills in different working environments. I value continuous learning and want to turn technical knowledge into practical, well-documented projects.

My goal is to grow into an Industrial IT professional who can help connect business IT infrastructure with operational technology.
